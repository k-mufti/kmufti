# Infinite Kitchen - the places and the odd ones out.
#
#  - The Ocean salts or pickles what it touches (it's brine): cheese -> Feta,
#    greens -> Seaweed, vegetables -> Pickle, bread and flour -> Mush, fruit
#    floats up as Coconut, and anything that lives in the sea stays itself.
#  - The Desert sun dries or bakes: fruit -> Dried Fruit, herbs -> Spices,
#    milk -> Milk Powder, broth -> Bouillon, eggs fry, potatoes bake.
#  - A Garden, Bush or Grass grows what it could grow and composts the rest.
#  - Vinegar kills weeds (Soil); salt ruins ground (Desert).
