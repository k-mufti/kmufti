# Infinite Kitchen - depth-3 rows: the starters against everything within
# three steps. "@ Item" starts a row; "Partner = Result" means Item + Partner.
#
# Families kept the same across every row:
#  - Soil + a food it could grow from grows it (fruit -> Tree, vegetables ->
#    Garden); Soil + any other food is Compost; Soil + sweets is Dirt Cake.
#  - Water rehydrates dried things (Jerky -> Meat, Dried Fruit -> Fruit),
#    thins thick ones (Condensed Milk -> Milk), turns raw meat into Broth,
#    and does nothing to an animal or a vegetable - so they come back.
#  - Meat + an animal is its meat; Meat + a place is the animal living there.
#  - Egg + a green is a Frittata, + a herb or filling an Omelette.
