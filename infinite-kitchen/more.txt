# Infinite Kitchen - the second layer. Same format as opening.txt.
#
# "X is Y" means X is really Y underneath: when a technique meets X and
# nobody wrote that pair, it's cooked the way Y would be. Salted Egg is Egg,
# so Salted Egg + Heat = Fried Egg. Only for things that truly ARE the other
# food with a small difference - never for a whole new food.
#
# Consistency notes for this layer:
#  - An animal next to food acts like what it gives: Cow is milk and beef,
#    Hen is chicken, Pig is pork, Bee is honey.
#  - Two land animals make a Farm. Land animal + fish is Surf and Turf.
#  - Meat + baby animal grows it up. Meat + grown animal is its meat.
#  - A place + heat: Ocean gives Salt, Soil and Field dry into Desert, and
#    the Desert is already as hot as it gets.
#  - "Nothing happens" only when it's true: Water + Whip, Ocean + Cut.
