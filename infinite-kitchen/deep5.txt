#    + yogurt Raita, + flour Pakora, + pasta Primavera, + cream Chowder.

#  - Caramel + nuts is Praline, + seeds Brittle, + butter Toffee, + fruit a
#    Candy Apple, + something sour Sweet and Sour Sauce.
#  - Seafood + something sweet is Honey Walnut Shrimp, + rice Paella,
#    + potato or corn a Seafood Boil.
