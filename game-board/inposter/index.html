Inposter

Everyone gets the secret word.
Except one of you.

  1. 1
    Peek at your card. It shows the word, or tells you you're the inposter.
  2. 2
    Give one-word clues, in turn. Prove you know it, without giving it away.
  3. 3
    Argue, point fingers, vote. Catch the one who's faking it.
  4. 4
    Caught? The inposter gets one guess at the word to steal the win.
