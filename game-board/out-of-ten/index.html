Out of Ten
  1. One of you sits in the middle. Everyone else is told a number from 0 to 10. You aren't.
  2. Pick a card like "Fast food". It goes to the next player round the circle, who gets 30 seconds to name one that's that number out of ten.
  3. After every answer, say where you're at. "I'm at a 7." Moved you closer? They score, but nobody finds out till the end.
  4. Everyone gets a card, then one final guess. Spot on is 5 for you and 1 for everyone.
