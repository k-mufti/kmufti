21 Questions

One of you thinks of something.
The rest get 21 questions.

  1. 1
    The answerer types a secret. Any person, place or thing.
  2. 2
    Take turns asking yes or no questions.
  3. 3
    One final guess each. Wrong, and you're out of guesses.
  4. 4
    Guess it, you win the round. Nobody does in 21? The answerer wins.
