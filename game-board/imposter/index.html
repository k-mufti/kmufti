Imposter

Everyone gets the secret word.
Except one of you.

  1. 1
    A reel picks the category. Everyone sees it. Don't like it? Most of you can vote to spin again.
  2. 2
    Peek at your card. It shows the word, or tells you you're the imposter.

How it plays

  1. The reel picks a category each round. A majority of you keeps it or spins again.
  2. Everyone gets the secret word, except the imposter, who only knows the category.
  3. Three rounds of typed clues. One word each, three at most, never the word itself.
  4. Seen enough? Tap “Vote now”. When 75% of you have, the voting starts early.
  5. 90 seconds to talk it out, then everyone votes. Most votes is out.
  6. Caught? The imposter gets one guess at the word to steal the round.
