Alfred — Privacy Policy

last updated 23 november 2026

What Alfred is

Alfred is a personal productivity tool, a Telegram bot that plans my day and keeps track of my job applications. It is built and used only by me, Kareem Muftee. It is not offered to the public.

Google data Alfred accesses

  • Gmail (read-only). Alfred reads the sender, subject and preview of recent job-search emails. It uses them to count job applications and to notice recruiter replies, interviews and rejections. It cannot send, modify or delete email.
  • Google Calendar. Alfred reads events to find busy time. It creates planning blocks, and it removes only the planning blocks it added itself.

Where the data goes

  • Data is stored in a private database on my own machine or server.
  • Email sender, subject and preview are sent to an AI model (through OpenRouter) only to classify them.
  • Nothing is sold or shared for advertising, and I don't use any of it to train models.

Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Anyone can remove Alfred's access to their Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
